Display Torch turns your screen into a soft, adjustable light: five brightness steps and a red night-vision mode. Tap to cycle brightness, two-finger tap for red light, hold to fine-tune and reach options — a first-run tutorial (reopenable from the ⋮ menu) walks you through it. A Quick Settings tile launches it from the lock screen. Fully usable with TalkBack.

Free, with a small banner ad; a one-time "Remove ads" purchase is available.
